Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

In short: Weekly Plant doesn't collect, share or sell any of your data. Everything you log stays on your device.

What the app stores

Weekly Plant saves the plants you log, the weeks you log them in, and your settings, such as your weekly goal, the day your week starts and your tracking timezone. This data is stored only on your device.

What the app doesn't do

  • It doesn't require an account or ask for your name, email or other personal information.
  • It doesn't send your data to the developer or any third party.
  • It doesn't include analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • It doesn't use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Backups

If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, your app data may be included in that backup. Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete older weeks from the app's Settings. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Weekly Plant doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
